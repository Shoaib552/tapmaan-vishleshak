Tapmaan Vishleshak Knowledge Base:

AIR QUALITY (AQI) LEVELS & SAFETY:
- Level 1 (Good): Safe for all outdoor activities.
- Level 2 (Fair): Generally safe.
- Level 3 (Moderate): Sensitive groups should wear masks.
- Level 4 (Poor): Unhealthy air. Avoid outdoor walking or exercise. Everyone MUST wear a mask.
- Level 5 (Very Poor): Health alert. CRITICAL DANGER. Mandatory N95 masks. Stay indoors.

DECISION RULES FOR OUTDOOR ACTIVITY:
- If AQI is Level 4 or 5 → VERDICT: "AVOID"
- If Temp is 30°C - 36°C → VERDICT: "LIMITED EXPOSURE"
- If Temp >= 37°C → VERDICT: "AVOID"

HEATWAVE SAFETY:
- Temperatures above 40°C are dangerous.
- Stay hydrated and avoid direct sunlight between 12 PM and 4 PM.

THUNDERSTORM SAFETY:
- Avoid open areas like parks or fields. Stay indoors.
- Disconnect electrical devices.
